daily loop — you scroll past things worth keeping on every platform,
bookmarker captures them in two taps, files them for you, and gives them

## 1. Email to 9to5Mac's Indie App Spotlight

**To:** tips@9to5mac.com
**Subject:** Indie App Spotlight tip: bookmarker — one library for everything you save on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube

Hi Michael,

Quick tip for Indie App Spotlight. I'm the solo developer of **bookmarker**,
a bookmark manager that shipped on the App Store on 2 September (free,
iOS 17+, 8MB). You covered Laytr on 29 August for the same "everything you
save, in one place" space; bookmarker's angle is the daily habit rather
than the archive.

The problem it's built around: you scroll past a dozen things a day worth
keeping — a recipe on Instagram, a thread on X, a workout on TikTok — and
every platform's Saved list swallows them: no titles, no search, no order,
and nothing you saved in one app is visible from another. bookmarker sits
above all of them. Share to it from any app (two taps) and the save is
filed into a topic on its own — fifty topics, on-device keyword matching
first, a private server-side model only for the links that need it. Then
it gives things back: search across titles, tags, notes and the account
something came from, and "side quests" that turn a pile of saves into a
small to-do (five recipes to cook, three places to visit).

The rest, briefly: it syncs to a web app at bookmarker.lol that works
without installing anything (there's a demo library to click around, and a
Revisit tab that surfaces what you saved and never opened). If someone has
years of old saves, it can also read the data export Instagram, TikTok, X
or YouTube already let you download, or a browser bookmarks file, and file
those too. The app asks for zero permissions and has no ads, no tracking,
no third-party SDKs.

## 4. One-paragraph boilerplate

bookmarker is a bookmark manager for iPhone and web that solves the thing
every social app gets wrong: saved posts that pile up with no titles, no
search and no order. Save from any app's share sheet and it's filed into a
topic automatically — fifty topics, over six hundred subtopics — and fully
searchable by title, tag, note or the account it came from; "side quests"
turn a pile of saves into a small to-do. It syncs between iPhone and a web
app at bookmarker.lol, asks for zero permissions, and carries no ads, no
tracking and no third-party SDKs. Existing saves can be brought in from the
data export Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube already provide, or from a
browser bookmarks file. Built by a solo developer using AI coding agents;
launched 2 September 2026.
