# 30-day launch calendar (2 Sep – 1 Oct 2026)

One weekly filming session (20 minutes, iPhone only — see `scripts/`)
produces four cuts from that week's hook family; those four rotate across
TikTok/Reels/Shorts through the week, with a repost of the strongest two on
the quieter back half. X gets a native post most days; Threads mirrors it.
Reddit reply templates (`posts.md`) are opportunistic — post one into a
**live** matching thread near the date shown, don't force it onto a dead
one. Every bookmarker.lol link carries
`?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=<video|post|reply>&utm_campaign=<slug>`.

Backlog note: each family has 8 hooks in `hooks.md`; this calendar only
spends 4 per family (32 of 40 total). The other 8 hooks (1.5–1.8, 2.5–2.8,
3.5–3.8, 4.5–4.8) plus all of Family 5's 5.5–5.8 are reserve — swap one in
if a scheduled day underperforms, or reuse the whole set as month 2's
calendar with fresh footage.

## Show HN text (Tuesday, Week 2 — 15 Sep)

**Title:** Show HN: I import your Instagram/TikTok/X saves into one searchable app

**Body (2 paragraphs):**

I got tired of "saving" things on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube and
never finding any of it again — every platform's saved list is a flat,
unsearchable pile with no titles and no order. I built bookmarker: save
from any app's share sheet and it's automatically filed into a topic (a
50-topic taxonomy, ~600 subtopics, keyword-matched on-device first, with a
server-side LLM fallback for anything ambiguous), then fully searchable by
title, tag, note, or the account it came from. The part I think is actually
interesting from an engineering angle: bulk import reads your platform's
own data export (Instagram's Download Your Information JSON, TikTok's data
export, X's archive, Google Takeout for YouTube, or a plain browser
bookmarks HTML file) and turns years of existing saves into a sorted
library in one pass — no scraping, no API keys, no ToS issues, since it's
all user-initiated exports of the user's own data.

It's a SwiftUI/SwiftData iOS app plus a no-framework single-file web app,
both talking to a small Supabase backend (Postgres + row-level security,
edge functions for the AI filing fallback via Claude/OpenRouter, never
called client-side). Everything works fully offline and signed-out on
iPhone; signing in just adds backup/sync to the web app. No ads, no
tracking, no third-party SDKs in the app. I'm a solo developer and built
this with AI coding agents end to end — happy to talk about that process,
the App Store review back-and-forth, or the import/filing internals in the
comments. Free: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6799805479 · web (no install,
has a demo library): https://bookmarker.lol/?utm_source=hackernews&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=show-hn
