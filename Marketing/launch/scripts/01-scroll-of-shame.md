# Script 1 — "POV: 2,000 saved reels" (the scroll of shame)

Week 1 filming session. Cuts hooks 1.1–1.4 from `hooks.md` (1.5–1.8 stay as
backlog for a weak day or month 2). UTM campaign: `scroll-of-shame`.

## B-roll to capture (one 20-minute iPhone session)

Record each clip 2–3× so there's a clean take to cut from. Screen recordings
via Control Center; do the share-sheet and search shots for real, on your own
library, no staged data.

| # | Clip | How | Length to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scroll through Instagram's Saved grid, thumb visibly moving, no pausing | Screen recording | 45–60s |
| 2 | Same, on TikTok's Favorites tab | Screen recording | 30s |
| 3 | Open bookmarker → Search tab → type a real query ("hip," "pasta," "marathon") → result appears | Screen recording | 20s ×3 (one per query you'll use) |
| 4 | Tap a search result → detail view → "Open original" → hands off to the source app | Screen recording | 15s |
| 5 | Inside Safari or YouTube: tap Share → scroll to bookmarker → tap it → "Bork'd to [Topic] › [Subtopic]" toast appears | Screen recording | 15s ×2 |
| 6 | bookmarker's You tab, showing save count and an "opened 9×" style stat | Screen recording | 10s |
| 7 | Face-to-camera cold open, 3–4 seconds, saying the hook's first line straight to lens (reuse across all 4 cuts, only mouth movement differs — record once per hook) | Front camera | 5s ×4 |

Total raw footage: ~5 minutes. Editing (CapCut or iMovie, cut to the shot
lists in `hooks.md`): budget the other 15 minutes of the session for that,
same day, while it's fresh.
