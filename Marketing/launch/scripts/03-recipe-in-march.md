# Script 3 — "That recipe I saved in March" (tired, diagnostic opener)

Week 3 filming session. Cuts hooks 3.1–3.4 from `hooks.md` (3.5–3.8 stay as
backlog). UTM campaign: `recipe-in-march`.

This family leads with fatigue, not outrage — the tone is "I'm tired," not
"this is broken." Keep the delivery flat and a little deadpan; the payoff
(instant search, with your own note attached) does the work.

## B-roll to capture (one 20-minute iPhone session)

| # | Clip | How | Length to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tired sigh, phone in hand, face-to-camera 3–4s (reuse across cuts) | Front camera | 5s ×4 |
| 2 | Scroll Instagram Saved, visibly slower/half-hearted than the Family 1 clips | Screen recording | 30s |
| 3 | Give up: back out of Saved, lock the phone / drop it on the couch | Screen recording + camera | 5s |
| 4 | bookmarker → Search → type one word ("pasta," "workout," "sister," "DIY") → result appears with its saved-date and note visible | Screen recording | 20s ×3 |
| 5 | Tap into the result, note text on screen, then "Open original" | Screen recording | 15s |

Total raw footage: ~3 minutes; the rest of the session is editing plus
picking three real items from your own library that actually have notes
attached (the note is the whole punchline — don't fake one).

## Notes
- Use three or four *real* saved items with real notes attached — this
  family's credibility rests entirely on the note/date being genuine, not
  a prop.
- Keep the energy tired, not funny — save the comedic beats for Family 2.
- If a comment asks "does it remind you to actually do the thing," the
  honest answer is no (that's resurfacing/digest, not built yet) — say so
  rather than imply it.
