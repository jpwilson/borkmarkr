# Script 2 — "I exported my Instagram saves and this is what came back" (JSON mess → sorted)

Week 2 filming session. Cuts hooks 2.1–2.4 from `hooks.md` (2.5–2.8 stay as
backlog). UTM campaign: `export-mess`. Import is iPhone-only today — every
shot here is a real iPhone screen recording, no mockups.

**Before filming:** request your own Instagram data export (Settings →
Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your
information → JSON format) a few days ahead — Instagram can take up to 48
hours to prepare it. Do this once and reuse the file across the whole week's
cuts.
