# Script 4 — "Pocket died. Here's where I moved six years of read-later"

Week 4 filming session. Cuts hooks 4.1–4.4 from `hooks.md` (4.5–4.8 stay as
backlog). UTM campaign: `pocket-died`.

**Critical honesty note:** bookmarker does not have a Pocket importer.
Pocket shut down in July 2025; there is no live Pocket export to demo against.
The entire family rests on one true, narrow claim: former Pocket users whose
reading list survived as **browser bookmarks** (the browser's own bookmarks
export, HTML format) can import that file into bookmarker today. Never say
"import your Pocket file," "Pocket import," or show a Pocket UI as if it
still exists and connects to bookmarker. If you personally never used
Pocket, say so on camera and film this as "for anyone who did" rather than a
first-person account — don't fabricate a personal history with the app.
