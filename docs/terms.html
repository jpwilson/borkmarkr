What bookmarker is

bookmarker lets you save links you choose to save, files them into topics, and makes them searchable. With an account, your saves are backed up and synced between your devices. bookmarker links out to the original pages; it does not host or reproduce their content.

Your account

You sign in with your email address (via a one-time code) or a supported sign-in provider. Keep access to that email or provider account secure — it is how your account is protected. You must be at least 13 to use bookmarker. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app; that permanently removes your account and everything backed up on our servers.
