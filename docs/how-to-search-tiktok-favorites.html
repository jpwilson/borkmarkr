How to search your TikTok favourites

Last updated: 6 November 2026

You saved the video — the pasta recipe, the shoulder exercise, the tip about renting a car abroad — and now you cannot find it. TikTok's Favorites tab is a grid of covers ordered by when you saved them, and the search bar at the top of the app searches all of TikTok, not your saves. This page covers what TikTok offers, the export route, and a way to keep saved TikToks somewhere you can actually search.

TikTok's menus change between versions. The paths below were right when this page was updated; if a label has moved, the same option is usually close by.

What TikTok gives you

Tap the bookmark icon on a video and it goes into Favorites on your profile. You can make collections there and move videos into them by hand. That is the whole system: no topics unless you make them, and finding one video usually means scrolling covers. With a few dozen favourites, that works. With a few hundred it does not.

Method 1: download your data and search the file

  1. In TikTok, go to Settings → Account → Download your data.
  2. Choose JSON and request the file; TikTok tells you when it is ready.
  3. Open user_data.json and look under your activity for the favourite videos list.

What you get: a link and a date for every favourite and liked video. What you do not get: captions, titles or creators — so you can find "something I saved on 3 May", but not "the pasta one". It is a snapshot, too, and does not update.

Method 2: keep saved TikToks in a library you can search

bookmarker is free and takes TikToks from the share button. On iPhone it reads each video's caption and creator from TikTok's public embed endpoint — no login, nothing scraped — so the words people actually write in a caption, hashtags included, become searchable, and the video's cover becomes the card.

  1. Get bookmarker. Install bookmarker free from the App Store (iOS 17 or later), or open the web app at bookmarker.lol.
  2. Share TikToks to bookmarker. On a video, tap Share, then the option that opens the iPhone share sheet, and pick bookmarker. Or tap Copy link and paste it in. On iPhone, bookmarker reads the caption and the creator from TikTok's public embed endpoint, so the caption, hashtags included, becomes searchable.
  3. File it in one tap. A dial offers the seven likeliest topics with the best guess in the middle; one tap files it, or leave it and the guess stands. Signed in, an AI model reads the caption and suggests a topic and subtopic.
  4. Import your existing favourites. Request your TikTok data (Settings → Account → Download your data, in JSON format), then drop the .zip into the web app's importer, or import the user_data.json inside it on iPhone. Favourites and liked videos both come across, with the date you saved each one.
  5. Search. Type a word from the caption, a hashtag, the creator, a tag or a note, or pick a topic. Tap the Topics, Subtopics or Tags chips to narrow a common word, and filter by source to see only TikTok.

What you can search, exactly

  • the caption, which becomes the save's title, hashtags included (videos shared from the iPhone app);
  • the creator;
  • the topic and subtopic the video is filed under;
  • your tags and dated notes;
  • the source app and the link.

Several words mean "all of these must appear". What search does not cover is what is said or shown in the video: bookmarker never fetches a transcript and does not download videos.

A word on imported favourites

An imported favourite arrives with what TikTok's export holds: the link and the date. Many export links are short share links with no creator in them, so imported favourites usually start as plain cards with no caption. They are still in your library and open straight in TikTok — and Sort your borks walks you through anything without a topic, one tap per card. For the ones you care about, a quick tag or note makes them as findable as a fresh save.

Tips that make the difference

Search the subject, not the words you half-remember

Because every save is filed into a topic, searching "mobility" finds videos filed under Fitness › Mobility even when the caption was a string of emoji.

Put TikTok next to everything else

The recipe you saved on TikTok and the one you saved on Instagram belong in the same place. In bookmarker both sit in Recipes, each labelled with its app, and opening either sends you back to the original.

Add a note for the ones that matter

A one-line dated note — "the garlic one", "do this before runs" — is the most reliable thing you can search for later, because it is in your words.

What this will not do

bookmarker keeps links, not copies: if a video is deleted or made private, the link stops working. It does not download TikToks or remove watermarks, and it cannot read your Favorites list on its own — the export is the way to bring that across. There is no Android app, but the web app runs in any browser.
