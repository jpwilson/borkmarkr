bookmarker vs mymind

Last updated: 21 October 2026

bookmarker and mymind start from the same good idea — you should not have to file the things you save — and then build two different products on it. bookmarker is a free iPhone and web library for posts you save out of Instagram, X, TikTok and YouTube, filed into a visible topic tree. mymind is a paid, private visual library for notes, bookmarks, images and inspiration, organised invisibly by AI.

Competitor details below are from each product's own website, checked on 21 October 2026, and they change. Follow the links and confirm anything that matters to you. Where a rival is better at something, it says so.

The core difference

mymind (per mymind.com) is "the extension for your mind": save anything with a click and its AI takes care of the rest, with no folders, labels or tags to maintain. It is deliberately private — their site lists no social features, no vanity metrics, no collaboration and no ads — and it leans visual, so a saved image, colour or product shot is something you look at rather than a row in a list.

bookmarker is about the pile of posts you already have in five different apps. It files every save into one of 50 topics and 612 subtopics, and it keeps the source app as a first-class dimension, so you can ask for "everything about running" or "everything I kept from TikTok" and get a straight answer. The filing is visible and editable rather than implicit.

Where each one wins

mymind is better if…

  • You collect visual material — images, type, palettes, products — and want a library that looks good.
  • You want notes, highlights and bookmarks in the same place rather than links alone.
  • You are on Android or work mostly in a desktop browser: mymind lists iPhone and Android apps plus Chrome, Edge and Safari extensions. bookmarker has no Android app and no browser extension.
  • You would rather never see a category at all.

bookmarker is better if…

  • What you save comes out of social apps on a phone, and you want the app it came from recorded and filterable.
  • You want to see and correct the filing — browse a topic tree, move a save, add your own subtopic.
  • You already have thousands of saves to bring over: bookmarker imports browser bookmarks and the X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube data exports.
  • You want the thing to be free.

Can you use both?

Easily, because they collect different things. mymind for the visual scrapbook, bookmarker for the stream of posts your phone throws at you. Neither one tries to be the other.

The bottom line

Pick mymind if you are building a beautiful, private collection of things you want to look at, and you want zero structure. Pick bookmarker if your problem is thousands of saved posts scattered across Instagram, X, TikTok and YouTube, and you want them in one free library you can actually search and browse by topic.
