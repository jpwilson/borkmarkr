borkmarkr — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

borkmarkr is a native iOS app that saves links on your phone. This policy is short because the app does very little with your data: everything stays local.

What the app stores

For each link you save, borkmarkr stores the URL, a title, which platform it came from, a topic and tags, any note you write, a thumbnail when one is available, the date you saved it, and how many times you have opened it.

Where it is stored

Only on your device. There is no sign-in and no account. Your library is not backed up to a server and is not synced to other devices. Nothing you save is sent to us, and we cannot see your library.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no cloud sync, and no sharing of your library.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Automatic filing

borkmarkr files saved links into topics on your device, offline, from the link itself. Nothing is sent to a language model or any other remote service to classify a save.

Requests to other websites

When you save a link, the app may request that page directly from your device to read its title and preview image. That website therefore receives an ordinary web request from your device, as it would if you opened the link in a browser. borkmarkr does not tell those sites who you are.

Deleting your data

Deleting a saved link removes it from your library. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. There is no server copy to delete.

Children

borkmarkr is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above.
