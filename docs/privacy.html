borkmarkr — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

borkmarkr saves links you choose to save. This policy describes exactly what is stored, where, and who can see it. It is short because the app does very little with your data.

What the app stores

For each link you save, borkmarkr stores the URL, a title, the name of the account or site that posted it, which platform it came from, a topic and tags, any note you write, a thumbnail image address, the date you saved it, and how many times you have opened it.

Where it is stored

By default, only on your device. If you never sign in, nothing you save leaves your iPhone. There is no account, and we cannot see your library.

If you sign in, your saved links and your email address are stored on servers operated by Supabase in the United States, so that your library is backed up and available on your other devices. Access is restricted at the database level: only your account can read or write your rows.

Your email address

Signing in is optional. If you do, we store your email address for the sole purpose of identifying your account and sending you a one-time sign-in code. There is no password. We do not send marketing email.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Automatic filing

borkmarkr sorts saved links into topics. Almost all of that happens on your device, offline, by matching words in the title and address.

When that isn't enough to place a link and you are signed in, the app asks a language model instead. In that case the link's address, its title, the account that posted it, and any tags you typed are sent to our server and from there to a language model provider (currently Anthropic's Claude, accessed via OpenRouter), which returns a suggested topic. These providers process the request on our behalf and do not use it to train models. Your notes and your email address are never sent. If you are signed out, this never happens.

The suggestion is only a suggestion: it is shown to you and you can change it before saving.

Library highlights

If you are signed in, the app can summarise what you have been saving lately ("what's interesting"). To do that, it sends the titles, topics and tags of up to 40 of your recent saves — never the links themselves, never your notes — to the same language model provider, which returns a short summary. This only runs inside the app for you; the result is not stored on our server and is not visible to anyone else. Signed out, this feature is off.

Requests to other websites

When you save a link, the app requests that page directly from your device to read its title and preview image. That website therefore receives an ordinary web request from your device, as it would if you opened the link in a browser. borkmarkr does not tell those sites who you are.

To draw a small site icon next to each save, the app also asks Google's public favicon service for the icon of that site's domain — the domain only, never the full link, and nothing about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting a saved link removes it from your library. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

To delete your account and everything stored on the server, email jeanpaulwilson@gmail.com from your account's email address, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

borkmarkr is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above.
