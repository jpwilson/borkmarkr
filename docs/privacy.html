bookmarker — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

bookmarker saves links you choose to save. This policy describes what is stored, where, and who can see it.

What the app stores

For each link you save (a “bork”), bookmarker stores the URL, a title, the name of the account or site that posted it, which platform it came from, a topic and tags, any note you write, a thumbnail image when one is available, the date you saved it, and how many times you have opened it.

Some sites hand out thumbnail links that stop working after a few days. When you are signed in and a save carries one of those, our server fetches that thumbnail once and keeps a copy so your library keeps looking right. The copy is stored alongside your other data and is deleted with your account.

Where it is stored

By default, on your device. If you never sign in, your library stays on your iPhone and we cannot see it.

If you sign in, your saved links and your email address are stored on our servers in the United States, so your library is backed up and available on your other devices. Access is restricted so only your account can read or write your data.

Your email address

Signing in is how we verify who you are. We store your email address for the sole purpose of identifying your account and sending you a one-time sign-in code. There is no password. We do not send marketing email.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the iPhone app.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Automatic filing

bookmarker sorts saved links into topics. Almost all of that happens on your device, offline, by matching words in the title and address.

When that is not enough to place a link and you are signed in, the app may ask a language model instead. In that case the link’s address, its title, the account that posted it, and any tags you typed are sent to our server and from there to a language model provider acting on our behalf, which returns a suggested topic. That provider does not use the request to train models. Your notes and your email address are never sent. If you are signed out, this never happens.

The web app at bookmarker.lol has no offline filer, so when you are signed in there, every link you save is filed this way straight away — the same four things are sent, and nothing else.

The suggestion is only a suggestion: it is shown to you and you can change it before saving.

Library highlights

If you are signed in, the app can summarise what you have been saving lately. To do that, it sends the titles, topics and tags of up to 40 of your recent saves — never the links themselves, never your notes — to that same language model provider, which returns a short summary. This only runs inside the app for you; the result is not stored on our server and is not visible to anyone else. Signed out, this feature is off.

Requests to other websites

When you save a link, the app may request that page directly from your device to read its title and preview image. That website therefore receives an ordinary web request from your device, as it would if you opened the link in a browser. bookmarker does not tell those sites who you are.

If you are signed in, our server may make the same kind of request — to read a page’s title and preview image, or to fetch a thumbnail that would otherwise expire. Those requests come from our server, identify themselves as bookmarker, and carry nothing about you.

To draw a small site icon next to each save, the app may also ask Google’s public favicon service for the icon of that site’s domain — the domain only, never the full link, and nothing about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting a saved link removes it from your library. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

To delete your account and everything stored on the server — your library, thumbnails and any feedback you sent while signed in — email hello@bookmarker.lol from your account’s email address, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

The website

If you leave your email address on bookmarker.lol to hear about the beta or the launch, it is stored on our servers and used only to email you about bookmarker. No one else can see the list. To be removed, reply to any of those emails or write to hello@bookmarker.lol.

The website and web app use PostHog (hosted in the United States) for product analytics and session replay, so we can see where people get stuck and fix it. Replays mask everything you type. There are no advertising trackers, and none of this data is sold or shared.

If you send feedback from the Help tab, the message is stored on our servers together with what you were using at the time (web or mobile, screen size, browser) so bugs can be reproduced. If you are signed in it is tied to your account and deleted with it; if you are signed out, the email address you may leave is used only to reply to you. Feedback is read by the person who builds bookmarker and no one else.

Children

bookmarker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above.
