How bookmarker works

  1. Bork it. Share from Instagram, X, TikTok or YouTube to bookmarker on your iPhone, or tap + here and paste a link. Saving is instant — nothing to fill in.
  2. It files itself. Every bork lands in a topic, and usually a subtopic, on its own. Open a bork to change where it lives, add tags or a note.
  3. Find it in seconds. Browse by topic or by source, or search every word you've ever borked — titles, captions, notes, tags.
  4. Side quests. Group borks around something you're actually doing — run a faster 10k, pick a van — and tick off the steps.
  5. It's yours. Sign in with a one-time code, no password. Your library syncs between the iPhone app and this site, and you can delete everything from the You tab.
