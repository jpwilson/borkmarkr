borkmarkr

The folder your feeds never gave you.

Instagram has nothing to do with YouTube Shorts. Shorts have nothing to do with X. You don’t care where something came from. You want the useful thing back.

Platform bookmarks are a write-only graveyard. Nothing is titled. Nothing is ordered. Nothing remembers that you opened it nine times. borkmarkr is the layer above all of them.

A saved item is a bork.

You bork something. Then you can search it, file it, and open the original. “412 borks.” That’s the product.

Where did I see that thing

  • Paste any link. It is saved with a title and filed on the phone.
  • Fifty topics, about six hundred subtopics. Change it if the guess is wrong.
  • Browse by topic, or by the app it came from.
  • Search titles, tags, notes, and people. A Related strip finds what you meant, not just the words you typed.
  • Dated notes, plus how many times you actually went back.
  • Open a bork and you leave the app — we store the link, not the post.
