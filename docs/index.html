The problem

Saved everywhere. Findable nowhere.

A reel on Instagram, a thread on X, a clip on TikTok, a video on YouTube — every app keeps its own saved list. None of them are titled, sorted or searchable, so you never find any of it again.

bork noun, verb

A saved item. You bork something. Then you can search it, file it, and open the original.

“412 borks.” That’s the product.

What it does

One library above all of them.

Saving is instant. Organising is optional — borkmarkr does most of it for you, and you can change anything it gets wrong.

Save from anywhere

Tap Share in any app and pick borkmarkr, or paste a link. The title, the account that posted it and a thumbnail come along automatically.

It files itself

Every bork lands in a topic and subtopic — Fitness › Mobility, Recipes › Meal prep. Fifty topics, six hundred subtopics, and you can add your own.

Browse two ways

By topic, to see everything about running no matter where it came from. Or by app, to see everything you kept from TikTok. Each view filters the other.

Find it again

Search titles, tags, notes, people and links. A Related strip surfaces the near-matches — what you meant, not just the words you typed.

Notes, and a memory

Add a dated note to any bork so you remember why you kept it. borkmarkr also counts how many times you’ve gone back to it.

Side quests

Running a marathon, choosing a car, planning a trip? Group the links that help with the goal, and add a daily habit if it needs one.

    Filed
