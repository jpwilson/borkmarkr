bookmarker vs X (Twitter) built-in bookmarks

Last updated: 21 October 2026

X has a bookmark button, it is free, and it is one tap. So why add anything? The short answer: X's bookmarks only ever hold X, and they tell you nothing about what you saved or whether you ever went back to it. bookmarker is a free iPhone and web library that holds your X saves and your Instagram, TikTok and YouTube saves, files each one into a topic, and lets you search and annotate the lot.

X's bookmark features change, and some of them depend on a paid X Premium tier — bookmark folders, for instance, have been a subscriber feature. Check X's current help pages rather than trusting any comparison, including this one, on the detail. Everything below is about what a separate library adds, which does not change.

What X's built-in bookmarks are good at

Zero friction. You are already in X, you tap the bookmark icon, it is saved, there is nothing to install and nothing to pay. For something you intend to read tomorrow, nothing is better. If you save a handful of posts a month and only use X, you do not need anything else — and this page is not going to pretend otherwise.

What they cannot do

1. They only hold X

This is the big one. Your Instagram saves, TikTok favourites and YouTube "Watch later" are three more separate lists in three more apps. Nothing in X's bookmarks can see them, so the recipe you saved on Instagram and the recipe thread you saved on X never sit next to each other.

2. There is no topic to browse by

X's bookmarks are a reverse-chronological list. You cannot ask for "just the coding threads" or "just the recipes" unless you have manually filed things into folders — and folders are a paid-tier feature and still manual. You remember what something was about, not when you saved it.

3. They do not tell you what you actually use

No dates to sort by, no note about why you kept it, and no record of how many times you have gone back to something. The platform has no reason to build that: its business is your next scroll, not your last one.

4. They stay inside X

You cannot send someone a curated set of twenty saved posts as one link, and you cannot keep a copy anywhere you control.

What bookmarker adds

  • One library across apps. X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Threads, Pinterest and ordinary web pages, each labelled with where it came from. Browse by topic or by source app; each view filters the other.
  • Automatic filing. Every save lands in a topic and subtopic — 50 topics, 612 subtopics, always overridable, and you can add your own subtopics.
  • Search. Over titles, the account that posted it, your tags, your notes, the topic and subtopic, the source app and the link, narrowable to topics, subtopics or tags.
  • Dated notes. Why you kept it, written down, with a date.
  • Revisit counts. bookmarker counts how often you reopen a save, so the things you actually use rise to the top.
  • Shareable collections. Turn a set of saves into a page at bookmarker.lol/c/… with an expiry of never, one day or ten days, and switch it off whenever.
  • Your old saves. Import X's data export, plus Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and browser bookmarks.
  • It is free. First 20 saves with no account; a free email sign-in makes it unlimited and backed up.

One thing to be clear about, because it is easy to oversell: bookmarker does not index the inside of the posts you save. A search matches the title, the account that posted it, your tags, your notes, the topic and subtopic, the source app, the link, and any description text the page published. You can narrow it to topics, subtopics or tags. Several words mean "all of these must appear"; there is no quoted-phrase operator.

bookmarker also stores links rather than copies, so it does not rescue a deleted post — if that is what you need, look at Twillot, which archives X posts.

When X's bookmarks are enough

  • You save a handful of posts and clear them out regularly.
  • X is the only app you save from.
  • You do not need to search or group what you saved.

When a separate library is worth it

  • You have hundreds or thousands of saves and find nothing.
  • You save from Instagram, TikTok and YouTube as well.
  • You want to browse by subject rather than scroll by date.
  • You want to know what you keep coming back to, or to send someone a set.

Plenty of people use both: X's bookmarks as a quick inbox, bookmarker for anything they want to keep.
